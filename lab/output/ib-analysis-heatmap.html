Note: Why Slope Was Removed

Previous versions of this analysis included midline slope (10:00-10:30) as a signal component.

The 10:00-10:30 slope is computed during IB2 itself (09:30-10:30). It is not a leading indicator — it uses data from the period being predicted. When the slope was instead measured over IB1 (08:30-09:30), a true leading window, it did not add predictive value: accuracy was 88-95% regardless of slope direction when IB1 broke only one side.

Price vs midline at 10:30 had the same problem — it is known only at the end of IB2, not before.

The simplified signal (IB1 outcome alone) captures the full predictive content. No additional filters improved it.
