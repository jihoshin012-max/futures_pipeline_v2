Entry: bar close, ddof=0, same-bar re-entry, reversal logic
Runtime: 2.2s

Step-up logic:
  After entry, if price reaches rolling mean (dynamic midline):
    Long: bar high >= mean -> step-up armed
    Short: bar low <= mean -> step-up armed
  Once armed, stop moves to entry +/- offset_ticks * tick_size
  Step-up stop checked real-time (bar high/low), exit at bar close
  Original outer stop still active before midline reached
  Step-up fires once per trade
