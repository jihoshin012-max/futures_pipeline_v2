NOTE: With N=2 circuit breaker, the per-side counter can only be 0 or 1
when entering a trade (at 2 the side is blocked). Therefore:
  - mult=1.5: floor(1 * 1.5^0)=1, floor(1 * 1.5^1)=1 -> qty always 1 (no effect)
  - mult=2.0: floor(1 * 2.0^0)=1, floor(1 * 2.0^1)=2 -> qty=2 after 1 consecutive stop
  - max=2 vs max=3: identical because max possible qty is 2 (consec can only be 0 or 1)
