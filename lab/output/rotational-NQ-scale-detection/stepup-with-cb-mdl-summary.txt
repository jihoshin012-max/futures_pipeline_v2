Config: LB=100, Inner=0.75, Outer=1.75, Qty=1 (no martingale)
Circuit breaker: N=2 (per-side, daily reset, forced opposite resets both)
Max daily loss: $2500 (track daily PnL incl costs, halt when <= -$2500, reset daily)
Data: NQ-250tick-calibration.csv (127567 bars)
Cost: $4.0/trade
NQ tick size: 0.25, tick value: $5.0
RTH: 09:30-15:45, Entry at bar close, ddof=0
Runtime: 6.7s

Step-up logic:
  After entry, if price reaches rolling mean (dynamic midline):
    Long: bar high >= mean -> step-up armed
    Short: bar low <= mean -> step-up armed
  Once armed, stop moves to entry +/- offset_ticks * tick_size
  Step-up stop checked real-time (bar high/low), exit at bar close
  Original outer stop still active before midline reached
  Step-up fires once per trade
  Step-up exit counts toward consec stop counter if PnL < 0
